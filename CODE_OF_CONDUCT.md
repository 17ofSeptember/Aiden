# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Aiden is an open technical project involving software, electronics, biosignals, and human-computer interaction. Project spaces should remain useful, respectful, and focused on productive collaboration.

## Expected Conduct

Participants are expected to:

- discuss technical disagreements without personal attacks
- provide criticism of code, designs, tests, and claims rather than criticism of people
- respect differences in experience and background
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or targeted abuse
- avoid posting another person's private information
- avoid publishing another person's biosignal recordings without their permission
- clearly distinguish tested behavior from assumptions or unverified claims
- avoid presenting Aiden as a medical diagnostic system

## Unacceptable Conduct

Examples include:

- harassment or intimidation
- discriminatory or demeaning comments
- sexual harassment
- deliberate disclosure of private information
- sustained personal attacks
- malicious disruption of project spaces
- knowingly false claims of testing, safety, or medical capability

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces operated for the Aiden project.
